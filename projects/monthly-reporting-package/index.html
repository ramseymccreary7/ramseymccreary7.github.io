December 2025 — Executive Commentary

Revenue: December revenue of $13.0M was a record month, exceeding budget by $2.5M (+24.4%) and up 24.3% vs prior year. Commission income drove the beat at $9.2M. HomeGo contributed $1.2M, well above the $111K budget as the partnership ramps.

Gross Margin: December gross margin of 60.4% was ahead of budget (58.3%) and significantly above prior year (56.4%), reflecting favorable deal economics and mix shift.

Operating Income: December OI of $2.6M was more than double the budget of $1.2M and up 36% vs PY. EBITDA of $2.5M represents a 19.5% EBITDA margin vs 12.0% budgeted.

Volume: 658 closings in December, 8.6% ahead of the 606 budget and 9.3% above PY. Average deal spread of $13,965 exceeded the $12,977 budget.

YTD: Full-year revenue of $132.3M was 5.8% below budget ($140.4M) but in line with PY ($138.6M). Full-year EBITDA of $16.8M (12.7% margin) is in line with budget ($16.3M, 11.6%) and slightly below PY ($20.1M, 14.5%).

Atlas Title: 348 deals in December vs 355 budgeted. YTD revenue of $8.5M in line with PY.

Atlas Lending: 66 loans originated in December vs 100 budgeted. YTD origination volume of $14.2M vs $23.3M budget reflects tighter deal economics. Interest income tracking at $4.6M YTD.
