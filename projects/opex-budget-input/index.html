Click a labor row to open Labor Detail, or any non-labor category to open it in Budget Input.

Shaded cells are inputs. Title is editable. Enter a term date for anyone leaving, or ✕ to mark them inactive. Enter the bonus as a % or a $ — the other calculates (the one you typed is outlined). Bonus frequency and criteria are required when there is a bonus; Custom needs an explanation. Raise % is pre-filled from last year's template — confirm or change it. Benefits are calculated from PY rates (see below the table).

Benefit rates come straight from the historical data: each benefit GL's TTM actual ÷ TTM gross pay (salary + bonus + commission) for the same calendar month. Every employee's benefits = their monthly pay × these rates, so raises, bonuses and new hires flow through automatically — and refreshing the actuals refreshes the rates.
