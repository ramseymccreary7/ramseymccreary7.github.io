Atlas $/Deal — Pure Forward Forecast

FY 2025 Predicted from December 2024 Baseline • Zero Concurrent 2025 Data

20 MODELS • 4 FEATURE TIERS • RECURSIVE PREDICTION
What is this?
We built a model that tries to predict Atlas's monthly $/deal for all of 2025 — using only information that was available on December 31, 2024. Think of it as standing at the end of 2024 and asking: "based on everything we know right now, what will our average spread look like each month next year?" The model learns patterns from six years of history (2019–2024) — things like how $/deal tends to behave from month to month, seasonal trends, and what macro-economic indicators (mortgage rates, housing starts, etc.) have historically correlated with our spreads. It then projects those patterns forward for 12 months. Critically, it uses zero actual 2025 data — no real deal results, no agent headcounts, no KPIs from the year being forecasted. The best model — a Random Forest — landed within $557/month of the actual result on average (about a 4% error rate), and had almost no directional bias — meaning it didn't systematically over- or under-predict. What's interesting is that this winning model uses the simplest set of inputs: just our own recent $/deal history (what happened last month, the last 3/6/12-month averages, and whether the trend is moving up or down) plus the time of year (seasonality). It doesn't need mortgage rates, housing starts, or any macro data — our $/deal's own momentum and seasonal rhythm are the strongest predictors. A Random Forest works by building hundreds of decision trees, each looking at the data slightly differently, and then averaging their answers — which makes it good at capturing non-linear patterns without overfitting. We tested 20 different model configurations across four tiers of input data to find the best approach.
What did we test?
Even though the best model ended up not needing macro data, we didn't know that going in. We tested 47 national economic indicators spanning every major category that could plausibly influence residential real estate investment spreads:

Housing Market (14 variables): 30-year mortgage rate, Case-Shiller Home Price Index, existing home sales, new home sales, housing starts, building permits, median home price, months supply of inventory, days on market, price cuts %, NAHB builder confidence, home price year-over-year change, price momentum (3-month), and building permits 6-month change.

Interest Rates & Credit (7 variables): Federal funds rate, 10-year Treasury yield, yield spread (10yr minus Fed Funds), real mortgage rate (mortgage minus inflation), mortgage rate 3-month change, mortgage affordability index, and bank lending standards from the Fed's Senior Loan Officer survey.

Consumer & Labor (5 variables): Unemployment rate, initial jobless claims, consumer sentiment (University of Michigan), wage growth year-over-year, and the CPI inflation index.

Financial Markets (4 variables): S&P 500 index level, S&P 500 year-over-year change, VIX volatility index, and DXY dollar index.

Construction & Materials (5 variables): Lumber price, lumber year-over-year change, PPI construction materials, construction cost year-over-year, and oil/WTI price.

Rental Market (4 variables): Zillow observed rent index, rent growth year-over-year, rental vacancy rate, and rent-to-price ratio.

Distressed & Investor (4 variables): Investor purchase share, foreclosure rate, foreclosure filings, foreclosure 6-month change, and 90+ day mortgage delinquency rate.

Broad Economy (4 variables): ISM manufacturing PMI, affordability proxy (rate × price composite), home price year-over-year, and HPI year-over-year change.

On top of macro, we also tested 41 internal Atlas operations variables (deal counts, agent headcount, acquisition/disposition prices, assignment vs. double-close mix, office concentration, deals per agent, and lagged versions of each) and 22 KPI variables (disclosures, offers, available inventory, conversion rates, agent productivity by type). In total, the analysis evaluated 122 potential predictors across 20 model configurations to find the most accurate and reliable approach.
Technical Detail: All models trained exclusively on Jan 2019 – Dec 2024 data (72 months). For each 2025 month, AR features (lags, moving averages, momentum) are computed recursively from predictions — not actuals. Seasonality is deterministic. Macro variables use 2025 actuals as a proxy for consensus forecasts. Internal and KPI features are frozen at their Dec 2024 values. No 2025 operational data is used at any point.
