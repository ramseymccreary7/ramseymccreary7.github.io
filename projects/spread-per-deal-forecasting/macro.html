⚠ Mortgage Rate — Inverse Relationship Confirmed

The current-month 30-year mortgage rate shows a weak inverse correlation (r = -0.12, not significant). However, the 6-month lagged mortgage rate IS statistically significant (r = -0.28, p = 0.016). This means when rates rise, $/deal drops — but it takes ~6 months for the effect to show up in deal economics. The Real Rate (mortgage minus inflation) has an even stronger inverse relationship (r = -0.57, p < 0.001).
